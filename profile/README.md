## The Ilefoastro Method

A three-step approach to documenting Yoruba family history:

1. **Record.** Interview elders, with their consent, and note who told you each fact and when.
2. **Check.** Compare each account with documents, notebooks, archives and other relatives, and label what cannot yet be supported.
3. **Preserve.** Keep the record in an organised digital file, with copies in more than one place.

The Method helps families organise their own oral history. It does not prove descent, rank or chieftaincy titles, and it does not replace government, court or palace records. Real family records are private and are never published without the family's written permission.

- Free working paper: [The Ilefoastro Method (Zenodo)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23289889)
- Method repository: [Ilefoastro-Method](https://github.com/ilefoastro/Ilefoastro-Method)
